## Setting and scope

1. **Map scale.** v1 has 20–30 plazas across the producing region, the border crossings and the US Southwest. *Default: 24 plazas.*
2. **Rival count.** v1 starts with 5 generated rival organisations of mixed size on the corridor, with splinters adding more. *Default: yes.*
3. **Agencies.** v1 has one fictional US-side federal agency; a producing-country agency arrives in v2. *Default: yes.*
4. **Real-world shocks after 2025.** Seeded only; no attempt to project real policy. *Default: yes.*
5. **Legitimacy exit in v1.** Scored but not playable until v2; v1 shows clean wealth as a score line. *Default: yes.*

## Difficulty and failure

6. **Difficulty levels.** Three (Lieutenant, Boss, Kingpin), setting agency budget, rival aggression and informant odds. *Default: yes.*
7. **Ironman.** Optional mode with one save and capture or death as final, as in TST. *Default: offered, off by default.*
8. **Capture.** On capture the player may play a successor (PL-0007); a captured leader can still influence the council from prison at reduced strength until convicted. *Default: yes.*
9. **Leader death.** Possible only through conflict or event outcomes at very high personal heat, always signposted by warnings first. *Default: yes.*

## People and council

10. **Informant visibility.** Suspicions appear as ranges after counter-intelligence work; the truth is revealed only by an event. *Default: yes.*
11. **Dealing with a suspected informant.** Choices are reassign, cut out of the loop, or expel; outcomes are reported, never depicted. *Default: yes.*
12. **Council size.** Grows with plazas held, capped at 9 seats. *Default: yes.*
13. **Tribute rule at start.** 20% of plaza earnings, adjustable each December. *Default: yes.*

## Economy and supply

14. **Named shipment threshold.** Shipments above a value threshold (scaled by stage) become named fixtures; below it, flow. *Default: about 4 named shipments a month at stage 3.*
15. **Price model.** Wholesale and street indexes by market follow TST's commodity walk, pinned to news-level estimates to 2025. *Default: yes.*
16. **Starting money.** A small dirty-cash stake and no clean book; the first front is the accountant background's perk or an early purchase. *Default: yes.*
