## PL-0005: Heat

- New system; Explain breakdown from TST `advisor/`

**Decision.**

1. Heat (0–100) is held at four levels: **person, site, route, organisation**. Organisation heat is a weighted roll-up, not a separate store.
2. Heat rises from activity: volume, violence outcomes, conspicuous spending, press attention, seizures and informant reports; it is lowered by concealment research, corruption reach and quiet months.
3. **Slow decay:** each month `H ← H − d × H`, with `d` small and lower at high heat, so a hot organisation stays hot.
4. Heat drives: the agency's choice of targets, raid odds, informant recruitment odds, rivals' targeting, and council confidence.
5. The player sees heat as bands with an Explain breakdown of its sources; the exact values stay hidden, as TST hides true attributes.

**Consequences.** Every growth lever has a visible cost; the core tension of the game is readable on one screen.
