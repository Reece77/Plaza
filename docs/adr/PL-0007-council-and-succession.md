## PL-0007: The council and succession

- Inherits: PT-0007 (confidence, votes, elections)

**Decision.**

1. The **council** is the plaza holders: lieutenants who hold territory. Each holds a confidence 0–100 in the leader, updated monthly by PT-0007's rule, with the target from their earnings against expectation, tribute fairness, organisation heat, losses, and their relationship with the leader.
2. **Tribute and splits** (PT's comp systems): each plaza holder pays tribute on their plaza's earnings under a rule the leader sets; generous splits buy confidence, harsh ones buy cash.
3. **The December council** decides tribute rules, expansion, wars and truces, by weighted vote; the player sees a predicted range and can lobby.
4. **Deposition:** when average confidence stays below 30 for six months, a challenger emerges. Outcomes: the player survives, is deposed and plays on as a plaza holder, or a crew splits away as a rival.
5. **Succession:** on the leader's capture, death or deposition, the council chooses a successor; the player may play the successor. **Game over** only with no successor or the organisation's collapse.

**Consequences.** Football Manager's dressing room again: the central skill is keeping earners loyal while heat rises.
