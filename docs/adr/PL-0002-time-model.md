## PL-0002: Time model — January 2006 start, monthly turns, a December cycle

- Inherits: TST ADR-0012 (12-phase turn), PT-0002 (start month as data)

**Decision.**

1. The campaign runs from January 2006 to December 2035 (360 turns). Turn 0 is data, so scenarios can start later.
2. **Monthly turns.** Shipments in transit, investigations and wars resolve across months; a critical event can pause the turn (TST's interruption rules).
3. **Named-versus-flow fidelity** (pattern of PT-0004): routine production and distribution resolve as flows each month; **named shipments** above a size threshold are individual records with a route, a crew and an outcome, the season's fixtures.
4. **The December cycle:** the council meets, tribute and splits are settled, the agency sets next year's budget and priorities, and rivals make their yearly decisions.
5. Phase order (draft): world and policy shocks → agency → rivals → production → logistics → markets and sales → laundering → heat → people (morale, growth, informants) → council → events → snapshot.

**Consequences.** Heat and investigations are slow, monthly accumulations, which suits a Football Manager pace; a raid is an event, not a real-time scene.
