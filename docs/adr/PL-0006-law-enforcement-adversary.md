## PL-0006: Law enforcement as an adversary

- Reuses: rivals' utility AI (PT); case machinery (TST regulation, PT risk)

**Decision.**

1. Fictional agencies, one in v1, are full simulated actors with a **budget**, **priorities**, a **research tree** (wiretaps, informant programmes, financial intelligence, interdiction technology) and a **political cycle** that changes budget and pressure.
2. Each December the agency picks targets by utility over heat, size, press attention and political value.
3. **Case machinery:** surveillance → investigation → raid or arrest → indictment → trial or plea, each a visible stage once discovered, with odds shown as ranges and warnings ahead.
4. **Informants** are recruited from members by heat, loyalty and pressure; a flipped member feeds the case and raises its odds. Informant status is a hidden value (TST ADR-0023): never visible through the API or a prompt until revealed by an event.
5. Eras (the kingpin strategy, extradition, encrypted-phone takedowns, sanctions) arrive as dated policy shocks to 2025, then seeded.
6. Agencies, officials and agents are generated; real governments appear only as sources of dated policy.

**Consequences.** The state learns and adapts; a strategy that worked in 2008 decays by 2015.
